Phase 1 of my QuantPath™ Mortgage Rate Intelligence Stack is complete.

**What I built:**
A reusable Excel financial modeling workbook that translates the FRED/Freddie Mac 30-year fixed mortgage rate series into practical household finance scenarios.

This isn't just a calculator — it's a decision-support tool that answers five business questions:

1. Where does the current mortgage rate sit relative to history?
2. How does a rate change affect monthly payments?
3. How does the rate environment affect lifetime interest cost?
4. How much loan can a fixed monthly budget support at different rates?
5. When does refinancing make financial sense, and how long to break even?

**Workbook structure:**
- Rate_Data → Assumptions → Rate_Shock_Model → Dashboard
- Amortization_Schedule and Refinance_Analysis feeding the same dashboard
- Configurable inputs so any borrower scenario can be modeled

**Key Excel functions used:**
`PMT`, `PV`, `CUMIPMT`, `PPMT`, `IPMT`, structured tables, named ranges, conditional formatting, dashboard charts

**Learning context:**
This project connects applied Excel-based analytics, financial modeling, and R-focused learning from IBM's Data Analytics with Excel and R Professional Certificate with my broader QuantPath analytics portfolio.

**What's next:**
Phase 2 will extend this into R-based time-series analysis of the mortgage-rate series — stationarity testing, rolling volatility, rate-regime analysis, and affordability implications by rate environment. This connects to my Applied Time Series Analysis coursework at North Carolina Central University.
